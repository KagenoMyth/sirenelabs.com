Hello, Sirene.

2026-09-09

The website is up, which means Sirene Labs is officially open.

Sirene Labs is an independent software laboratory based in Redmond, Washington. We build tools, systems, and ideas worth shipping — some for clients, some for ourselves, and as much of it in the open as makes sense.

A few things to expect from this space:

  • Build logs. Experiments in progress, described as they actually go — not the cleaned-up version.
  • Honest postmortems. When something breaks or a bet doesn't pay off, we'll write that down too.
  • Notes on craft. Occasional thoughts on building small, durable software.

What you won't find here: a content calendar, SEO filler, or posts written to hit a cadence. New writing appears when there's something worth saying — no more, no less.

If you want to follow along, there's an RSS feed. If you have a problem worth solving, get in touch.

— Sirene Labs
